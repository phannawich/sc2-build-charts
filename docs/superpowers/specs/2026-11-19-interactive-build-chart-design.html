03 / How a highlight behaves

InputResult
Hover or Tab focusPreview the card, its immediate predecessors and successors, and their connecting arrows. An existing pin stays in place.
Click, tap, Enter, or SpacePin or unpin the card’s highlight.
Escape or click empty chart spaceClear the highlight.
JavaScript unavailableAll chart text and arrows remain visible.

04 / What to check

  • Fidelity Compare every rendered card and arrow with the source.
  • Layout Check desktop and phone widths, including horizontal scrolling.
  • Controls Verify preview, pin, and clear with mouse, touch, and keyboard as applicable.
  • Offline Confirm that the site does not request the PNG.

Version history stays in local Git: no remote and no push.
